Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

CodeExecutor (the "Site") at codeexecutor.tech operates a remote code execution sandbox and related documentation. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Usage data (pages visited, timestamps, errors) collected automatically for analytics and debugging.
  • Optional user-submitted content such as code snippets or inputs you run in the sandbox. Do not submit secrets or personal data in code.
  • Cookies and local storage may be used to remember preferences (theme, language, editor state).

How We Use Data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its services.
  • To detect abuse, malware, or content that violates our policies.
  • To show non-personalized or personalized advertising through partners such as Google AdSense.

Ads and Third-Party Services

We may display ads provided by third-party networks (for example, Google AdSense). These providers may use cookies and other technologies to serve ads based on prior visits to the Site or other sites. We do not share personally identifying information with advertising networks.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data and execution environments. However, you should not submit passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information into the public sandbox.

Content Guidelines

Do not submit code that facilitates illegal activity, malware, exploits, or copyrighted material you do not own. Content that violates these rules may be removed and the submitting IP blocked.

Your Choices

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser to remove preferences and editor data. To opt out of personalized advertising, use the ad provider opt-out controls (for Google: adssettings.google.com).

Contact

For questions about privacy, email: admin@codeexecutor.tech
